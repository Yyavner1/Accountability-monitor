## What we collect

**Nothing from the Software's monitoring.** Accountability Monitor analyzes
your screen locally and sends reports **directly from your device** to the
recipients you choose. It does not send your screenshots, activity, or
personal data to us. We operate no server that receives or stores them.

## What leaves your device, and to whom

- **Alert and summary emails** go from your device (via the email account you
  configure) straight to the accountability partner you choose. We are not in
  that path.
- **Optional self-hosted dashboard:** if *you* choose to run the optional
  backend yourself, data goes only to the server *you* operate, not to us.
