Three layers

How it protects you

Each layer catches what the one before it can't — block first, blur what slips through, and report the rest to the person you've asked to keep you honest.

Blocks adult sites

A system-wide filter stops known adult sites from loading in any browser or app.

Blurs explicit images

A browser add-on blurs explicit images and video right on the page, as they load.

Watches the whole screen

For everything else, it checks the screen on your machine and reports flagged moments to your partner — with an alert if you try to switch it off.

    The one step people miss

    The Screen Recording permission. If your partner's reports come back empty or all-black, that permission isn't on yet — turn it on and reopen the app.

    About that Gmail App Password

    It's a one-time code that lets the app send email from your account without your real password. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, make one called "Accountability", and paste it into the settings page.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Is it really free?
    Yes. It's open-source under the MIT license, and the full code is public on GitHub. There's nothing to buy and no account to make.
    My browser or Mac says it might be dangerous — is it?
    No. New apps that aren't yet code-signed always trigger that warning, because the system doesn't recognize them yet, not because anything's wrong. The full code is public on GitHub for anyone to inspect. In your browser choose Keep; on a Mac, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway; on Windows, click More info → Run anyway.
    Why do I have to open the file myself?
    No website is allowed to run a program on your computer on its own — that's the main way viruses would spread, so every browser blocks it. Opening the download yourself is the one click that can't be skipped. Everything after it is guided.
    Does it catch everything?
    No tool does. It blocks a lot, blurs more, and reports the rest. The detection runs on-device and is best-effort — the honest trade for keeping everything private.
    Who can see my screen?
    Only the partner you choose, and only blurred images of flagged moments, sent directly to them. Not us — we run nothing and receive nothing.
